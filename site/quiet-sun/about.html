What this site is

A reading room for quiet-Sun maps from the Murchison Widefield Array, built from the 2013–2015 local-noon list. It is not a burst catalogue.

A day is listed if GOES was quiet and the MWA observed the Sun within 30 minutes of local noon. 238 of 305 days have a compact calibrator within 24 hours. Figure 4 of Sharma & Oberoi (2020) is the 3 December 2015 scan at 03:39 UT. That scan is not the noon pick in the table; the noon pick that day is ten minutes later.

Brightness temperature is given in million K. We do not quote a 2022 scale factor on these maps. The CASA path is SuperUniform snapshots, then the Mohan & Oberoi conversion, then the mean of the scan. P-AIRCARS is shown beside that path, not inside it.
