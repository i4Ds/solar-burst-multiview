Observation 1133149192, not the noon-catalogue pick that day. All panels solar north up. Brightness temperature, million K.

108 and 162 MHz: Rohit, our remake, and P-AIRCARS all recover the disk. Peak brightness temperatures agree to about ten percent at 108 MHz. At 162 MHz P-AIRCARS is fainter (0.88 against 0.99 million K). 217 and 240 MHz: P-AIRCARS did not recover the disk. Those columns are shown because that is what the pipeline produced, not because they are quiet-Sun maps.
